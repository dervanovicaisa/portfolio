Case 01 / Energy trading platform

Making sense of the rules behind energy trading.

As a web developer at ClickerTeam, I worked on the energy-trading part of a larger virtual power plant and electricity distribution platform. Before building a screen, I needed to understand what its figures meant, which rules applied and what each user was allowed to do.

Role & team
Web developer / ClickerTeam
Period
September 2024 – August 2026
Focus
Trading data, business rules & user access
01 / Context

A number on a screen needs a meaning.

The screens supported day-to-day energy trading: what had been bought and sold, how much energy was committed for each time period, at what price, and how actual delivery compared with the plan.

Showing those figures was only part of the job. I had to understand what each value represented, how the figures depended on one another and which business rules shaped what a user should see.

02 / Contribution

What I worked on.

  • Frontend features: built functionality for the energy platform, including role-based permissions and dynamic forms driven by category.
  • Requirements: clarified questions and edge cases with the team, improved Jira ticket descriptions, divided work into subtasks and proposed acceptance criteria.
  • API understanding: used Swagger/OpenAPI to see what data the backend provided and how it behaved.
  • Testing: checked different scenarios against the expected logic and supported investigation during testing and after release.
03 / Challenges

When the requirements did not cover a case.

Testing revealed scenarios the original requirements had not addressed, including permission issues. I did not decide the answer in the frontend alone. I raised those cases with the team so we could agree on what should happen before I implemented the behaviour.

The trading figures also needed careful interpretation. I used the API documentation and tested different scenarios to check that the screen matched the intended logic. After release, I helped investigate issues that only appeared in real use.

Illustrative questions

The decisions behind a trading screen.

These examples show the kinds of questions raised by the documented work. They are not claims about specific production incidents.

01 / Meaning

What does this figure represent?

If energy is shown for several time periods, which period does each figure belong to, and how should actual delivery compare with the plan?

02 / Access

Who can take this action?

If someone can view trading data but cannot change it, should an action be hidden or shown as unavailable?

03 / Agreement

What should happen in an unlisted case?

If a tested scenario is missing from the ticket, what should the interface do? Agree on the rule with the team before implementing it.

04 / Perspective

What I would bring to systems analysis.

This work taught me to connect API data with its business meaning, test assumptions and make missing rules visible to the team. I held a developer role, but the part I found most valuable was clarifying what the system should do before building it.
