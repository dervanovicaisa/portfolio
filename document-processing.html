Case 02 / AI document-processing platform

Understanding what a workflow rule should do.

At Uhura Solutions, I built frontend features for an AI-powered document-processing platform used in banking and financial services. In a configurable workflow, the difficult part was understanding what each condition meant, what depended on it and what should happen next.

Role & team
Web developer / Uhura Solutions
Period
February 2023 – September 2024
Focus
Workflow rules, validation & extracted data
01 / Context

A condition needs more than a label.

The platform processed documents and included a configurable workflow builder. A condition could be shown on screen, but that alone did not explain what it meant or how it affected the next step.

My work involved translating workflow rules, conditions and validation logic into application behaviour. To do that well, I needed to understand the dependencies behind a condition, not just how it should look in the interface.

02 / Contribution

What I worked on.

  • Frontend features: developed functionality for an AI-powered document-processing platform.
  • Workflow builder: contributed to the configurable workflow interface and translated rules and conditions into application behaviour.
  • Validation: worked with logic that determined how the application should respond to workflow conditions.
  • Investigation: examined how extracted information mapped into the application, including cases with duplicate occurrences.
03 / Challenges

Understanding what should happen next.

The difficult part was interpreting a condition in context: what it meant, what depended on it and what should happen after it applied. I followed that logic before turning it into frontend behaviour.

I also investigated extracted-entity mapping and duplicate occurrences, checking how information from a document was represented in the application.

04 / Perspective

What I would bring to systems analysis.

This work strengthened my habit of asking what a rule means before implementing it. In a Business Systems Analyst role, I would use the same approach to clarify conditions, dependencies and expected behaviour with the team.
